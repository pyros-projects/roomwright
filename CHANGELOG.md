# Changelog

What changed for a player, newest first. The game shows the same text: CHANGELOG on its title screen.

## 0.4: The screen burns (in progress)

- Every version keeps its own address, so an older one can be played next to the new one.
- A changelog: this file, and a screen in the game.

## 0.3: The card decides the run (2026-11-21)

- Doors cost more the deeper you are. A sealed door adds to the knocking: 11% on floor 1, 15% on floor 2, 17% on floor 3. The HUD and each card show it.
- Rooms do the work. A room's power is 0.85, 1.6 and 2.6 at one, two and three stars, resonance is +40% per link, and room power keeps pace with the monsters downstairs.
- Each floor's elite goes for the room you built up most and switches it off while it stands there.
- The Stairwell is a sharper choice. Its bar says how strong the warden would be now and what a fast kill pays. Late in the dread clock the knocking grows, and a warden gets faster the longer its fight lasts.
- A damage tally on the end screen and in the pause menu: which weapons, rooms and charms did the run's damage.
- Pocket Bombs are trimmed.
- Stakes: after a first win the hero screen offers a ladder of six steps, one more rule each.
- Each floor has its own music: a piece of 48 to 56 bars on the floor's old tune, darker the deeper you go.
- A tick in the music is gone.

## 0.2: The first public version (2026-11-20)

- Three floors, 36 room cards, 24 weapons with an evolution each, 27 charms, four delvers and a deck you build between runs.
- A 30-second intro film before the title.
- The first draft teaches the card in the game, and H explains a card again.
- Knock back: a new hand behind a door, paid for in danger.
- Walking with the mouse, and first hints for touch.
