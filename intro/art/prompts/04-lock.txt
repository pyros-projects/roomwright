SHOT 4, the lock. Extreme close-up of the lock of the sealed double door from the third reference image (shot 2): the same brass lock plate with the same shape and the same keyhole, sitting on the vertical seam between the two wooden door leaves, placed in the right half of the frame. The seam runs vertically through the plate; above and below the plate a thin red-pink glow leaks through the seam, and the keyhole glows faintly red-pink, as in the reference. Iron bands with round rivets cross the planks above and below the plate, as in the reference, partly visible at the top and bottom edges of the frame. Seen straight on, warm firelight from the left. The left half of the frame is plain dark brown wooden planks, left empty for an animated key. Do not draw a key and do not draw a hand.
