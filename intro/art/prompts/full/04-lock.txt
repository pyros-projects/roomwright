Full-bleed 16:9 film frame for the animated intro of Roomwright, a cozy 16-bit dungeon game. One single image, no borders, no panels, no text, no watermark.
Style: bold cel-shaded storybook illustration that will be converted to chunky pixel art. Clean dark outlines, flat colour shapes with two or three tones of shading, large readable silhouettes, strong simple lighting, no gradients, no fine texture, no photorealism.
The first reference image is the model sheet: use these exact designs for Pip (a small chibi child in a teal cat-ear hood, cream tunic with a yellow clasp, brown trousers), the golden key, the glint gems, the green slime, the Warden and the sealed door. The second reference image is a screenshot of the game: match its world (warm tan brick floors, a red rug with a small campfire, white stone pillars, wooden doors with iron bands in grey stone frames, a deep purple-navy void outside the rooms).
Palette: warm tan brick #e3bd88 and #a6713f, deep purple-navy shadows #1a1426 and #3b2d4f, teal #3fc1ad, gold #ffd95e, orange #f6923a, red #e8474c, cream #fff8e8.
