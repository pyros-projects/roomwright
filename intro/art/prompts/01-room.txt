SHOT 1, establishing. High three-quarter top-down view of one small square cellar room floating alone in a dark purple-navy void. Tan brick floor, a red rug in the centre with a small crackling campfire on it, four white stone pillars, and one heavy sealed wooden door in the middle of each of the four walls (top, bottom, left, right), each door shut tight with iron bands. Pip stands on the rug beside the fire, tiny in the frame, seen from above and behind, looking up at the top door. The firelight makes a warm pool in the middle; the corners and the doors fall into shadow. Quiet, cozy, a little lonely. The whole room fits in the frame with dark void around it.
