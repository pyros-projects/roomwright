SHOT 7, the Warden. Low angle, dark and dramatic but still cute: the Warden, a gigantic living wooden door with furious glowing eyes, heavy brows and a wide mouth full of square white teeth, looms out of the darkness and fills the upper two thirds of the frame, lit from below by red-orange light. Around its feet, a few small green slimes with dot eyes. In the bottom centre foreground, tiny Pip seen from behind, standing firm and holding the golden key up high; the key shines. Deep purple-navy darkness all around, red rim light on the Warden's planks and iron bands, a few drifting embers.
