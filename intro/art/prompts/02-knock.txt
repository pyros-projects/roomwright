SHOT 2, the knock. Low angle close-up of one sealed wooden double door in its grey stone frame, towering and filling most of the frame, seen straight on. Dust trickles from the lintel. A thin sinister red-pink glow leaks through the gap under the door and through the keyhole. In the lower left foreground, Pip seen from behind (only the back of the teal cat-ear hood and the shoulders), small and dark against the door, looking up at it. Warm firelight from behind the camera lights the lower part of the door; the top fades into purple-navy shadow. Tense but cute.
