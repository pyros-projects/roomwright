SHOT 6, the floor plan. Straight top-down view, like a board-game map, of a small dungeon of seven square rooms on a dark purple-navy void, arranged on a grid and joined by short open doorways. In the centre, the starting cellar room (tan bricks, red rug, campfire). Around it, each room clearly different in colour: a Fountain Court (blue water fountain, blue-grey stone), an Ember Hall (orange glowing embers and braziers, dark red stone), a Moss Garden (green moss and flowers), a Frost Grotto (pale icy blue crystals), a Dusty Library (bookshelves, purple carpet), an Old Vault (gold coins and a treasure chest). On some outer walls, sealed doors with a faint red glow. All rooms have the same size and thick dark-brown brick walls, evenly spaced: a clean, readable map. A tiny Pip stands in the centre room.
