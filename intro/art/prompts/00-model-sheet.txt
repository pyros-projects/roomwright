Character and prop model sheet for the animated intro of a cozy 16-bit dungeon game called Roomwright.
The first reference image shows the game's actual pixel sprites, the second shows a screenshot of the game. Redraw these exact designs as larger, more detailed illustrations in a bold cel-shaded storybook style that will later be converted to pixel art: clean dark outlines, flat colour shapes with two or three tones of shading, no gradients, no texture noise, no photorealism.

On one sheet, on a plain mid grey-blue background, evenly spaced, show:
1. PIP, the hero: a small chibi child with a big round head, wearing a teal hood with two cat ears (pink inside the ears), teal hair fringe under the hood, simple black dot eyes, pink blush cheeks, cream-white tunic with a small yellow clasp at the collar, brown trousers and boots. Show Pip three times: front view, side view, back view. Same proportions in all three: head about half the body height.
2. THE KEY: a chunky golden key with a round bow with a hole and two square teeth, dark outline.
3. A GLINT: a small glowing four-pointed orange-gold sparkle gem.
4. A SLIME: a round glossy green blob with two black dot eyes and a white highlight.
5. THE WARDEN: a monstrous living wooden door: arched top, vertical brown planks, two iron bands with rivets, two furious white eyes with heavy brows, a wide mouth full of square white teeth, a small brass keyhole.
6. A SEALED DOOR: heavy wooden double door with iron bands and a brass lock plate, set in a grey stone frame.

Colours: teal #3fc1ad, gold #ffd95e, orange #f6923a, warm tan brick #e3bd88 and #a6713f, deep purple-navy shadows #1a1426 and #3b2d4f, red #e8474c, green #58b848, cream #fff8e8.
No text, no labels, no watermark.
