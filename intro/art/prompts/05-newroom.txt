SHOT 5, the new room. View from behind Pip, who stands small in the bottom centre as a dark silhouette in an open doorway: the two wooden door leaves have swung wide open to the left and right edges of the frame. Beyond the doorway, a brand-new room is assembling itself in the void: the Fountain Court. A round stone fountain with bright blue water in the middle, pale blue-grey flagstones, mossy stone walls, cool teal light. At the edges of the new room, loose bricks and floor tiles still float in the dark void, drifting into place. Wonder and discovery: the cool teal glow of the new room spills through the doorway onto Pip and onto the warm brick threshold.
